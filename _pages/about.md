I am a Ph.D. student in the Bradley Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Virginia Tech](https://ece.vt.edu/) under the supervision of Professor [Tom Hou](https://www.cnsr.ictas.vt.edu/THou.html). My research lies at the intersection of wireless communications, optimization, and machine learning, with a focus on the design of intelligent resource allocation algorithms for next-generation wireless networks.

Prior to joining Virginia Tech, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Isfahan University of Technology [(IUT)](https://ece.iut.ac.ir/en), Isfahan, Iran, in 2021. 

My current work explores deep reinforcement learning (DRL) frameworks for URLLC/eMBB coexistence in 5G and beyond, real-time GPU-based system implementations, and scalable scheduling solutions within the Open RAN (O-RAN) architecture.
